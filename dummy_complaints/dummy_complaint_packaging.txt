We have received multiple complaints from our retail stores regarding Ibuprofen 400mg tablets, Batch Number IBU994. 
A significant portion of the packages have broken foil seals on the blister cards, leaving the tablets exposed to air. 

Manufacturing date was 10th October 2024 and the expiration date was 9th October 2026.
We have quarantined approximately 1200 affected blister packs in our central warehouse. Fortunately, no adverse events have been reported by patients, but the product is unfit for retail sale.
