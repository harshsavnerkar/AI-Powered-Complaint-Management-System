Dear Quality Assurance,

We are reporting a critical contamination issue in Sterile Normal Saline Vials (10mL), Batch Number SAL441. 
Our nurses noticed small, floating black particles suspended inside the liquid of several unused glass vials during routine preparation for patient IV infusion. 

The manufacturing date for this batch was 22nd July 2025 and the expiry date was 21st July 2027.
The affected quantity is 50 vials. Fortunately, these were spotted before administration, so no patients were injected with the contaminated solution. We have quarantined the box.
